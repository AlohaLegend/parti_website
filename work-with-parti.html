The project lifecycle

A flexible process with a clear backbone.

01 / Discover

Discovery + Strategy

We align on the audience, ambition, constraints, budget range, timing, and what success needs to feel like before meaningful design begins.

02 / Develop

Creative Development

We turn the brief into a spatial point of view through concepts, mood, guest journey, visual language, early feasibility, and an actionable scope.

03 / Produce

Production

Design moves into drawings, fabrication, vendors, permitting, technical planning, logistics, run of show, and the thousand details that make the idea real.

04 / Execute

Install + Experience

Our on-site team brings every piece together, protects the creative through install, supports the live moment, and closes the loop after strike.

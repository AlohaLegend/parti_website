Notice at collection and privacy

PARTI collects the identity and contact information, business details, experience, rates, availability, insurance and safety readiness, and reference information entered here. We use it to evaluate potential vendors and independent talent, contact you about possible projects, manage compliance and onboarding, prevent fraud, and maintain business records.

PARTI does not sell this information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Profiles that do not lead to an active engagement are intended to be retained for up to 24 months, unless a longer period is required for a dispute, security, or legal obligation. If engaged, relevant information may become part of PARTI's vendor, tax, safety, insurance, and project records and follow the applicable retention period. Contact hello@letsparti.co with a privacy request.

Independent relationship notice

This intake does not create an employment relationship, independent contractor relationship, promise of work, exclusivity obligation, or binding rate. Any project requires a separate written scope that identifies deliverables, payment amount or rate, payment due date, and other project terms.

Legal status depends on applicable law and the actual working relationship. Nothing in this intake asks you to waive rights, benefits, unemployment claims, workers' compensation rights, safety protections, or the right to raise a classification concern.
